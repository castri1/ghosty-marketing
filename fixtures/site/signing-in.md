Ghosty has no passwords — not as an option you skipped, but by design.
There's nothing to remember, nothing to reuse across sites, and nothing
that can leak in a breach. You sign in with something you already own:
your email inbox, your Google account, your GitHub account, or a passkey
on your device.

Your email address **is** your account. However you sign in, everything —
your apps, your team memberships, your invites — is tied to your verified
email, and every sign-in method that reaches the same address reaches the
same account.

## Email link (works for everyone)

Enter your email at [getghosty.dev](https://getghosty.dev) and we'll send
you a one-time sign-in link. Open it on the same device and you're in.

- Links expire after 15 minutes and work exactly once.
- The first sign-in **is** the sign-up — if the address is new to Ghosty,
  your account is created on the spot.
- Didn't get it? Check spam, then use **Resend link** on the sign-in page.

## Continue with Google

If you prefer, click **Continue with Google** and pick your account.
Google confirms your email address to us, and you land in the same Ghosty
account that address always names — use the link one day and the button
the next, it's the same account either way.

If your Google account's address is unverified, Google sign-in is refused;
use the email link instead (the link itself is the verification).

## Continue with GitHub

Building from a GitHub account already? **Continue with GitHub** signs you
in with it. We use the verified email address on your GitHub account —
your primary one if it's verified, otherwise any verified address — and
you land in the same Ghosty account that address always names. Your email
can stay private on your GitHub profile; sign-in still works.

If none of your GitHub addresses are verified, GitHub sign-in is refused;
verify one there first, or use the email link instead.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN —
one tap, no inbox round-trip. Passkeys live on your device (or in your
phone's or browser's built-in manager, which can sync them across your
devices) and only ever work on Ghosty's real sign-in page, so there's
nothing to type and nothing a fake page can trick out of you.

To set one up, sign in however you like, then go to **Settings →
Passkeys → Add a passkey** and follow your device's prompt. Do it once on
each device you sign in from — or let your passkey manager sync it for
you. Next time, click **Sign in with a passkey** on the sign-in page and
confirm with your fingerprint, face, or PIN. You land in the same account
as always.

Lost the device? Nothing is locked away: sign in with your email link (or
Google/GitHub) and remove that passkey from **Settings → Passkeys**.

## Signing out

Use **Sign out** from the account menu. Sessions also expire on their own
after 24 hours, so a forgotten library computer signs you out by itself.
